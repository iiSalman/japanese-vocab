Vinland Saga — Season 1

12 new words

N5noun
海うみ
sea, ocean
S1E1 — “ここらの海うみはな トルフィン 怖こわいぞ”
“The sea around here is frightening, Thorfinn.”
the sea is the show's central frontier — voyages, sailors, and Vinland itself all hinge on it.
N5noun
船ふね
ship, boat
S1E1 — “わしの船ふねに乗のるか”
“Want to ride on my ship?”
Leif's ship and Thorfinn's dream of sailing recur throughout the episode.
N5noun
村むら
village
S1E1 — “この村むらの人ひとじゃないわね”
“This person isn't from the village.”
the whole episode unfolds in this small Icelandic village.
N5noun
友達ともだち
friend
S1E1 — “すぐに友達ともだちになったよ”
“We quickly became friends.”
core everyday noun; here it frames Leif's tale of meeting the natives of Vinland.
N5adj-i
寒いさむい
cold (of weather/air)
S1E1 — “寒さむかったな ユルヴァちゃん”
“It was cold, huh, Ylva.”
the worsening cold is the episode's looming threat — frozen lands drive the talk of migration.
N5adj-i
暖かいあたたかい
warm
S1E1 — “ここよりも ずっと暖あたたかくてな”
“It's much warmer than here.”
the warm, bountiful Vinland is set against the freezing north — learn it as 寒い's opposite.
N5adj-i
強いつよい
strong
S1E1 — “ハラルドという強つよい男おとこが現あらわれた”
“A strong man named Harald appeared.”
strength — who is truly strong — is a question the whole series keeps asking.
N5verb
死ぬしぬ
to die
S1E1 — “６人ろくにんの仲間なかまは 皆みな 死しんだ”
“All six of my comrades died.”
essential verb; here it lands the weight of Leif's survival story on the ice.
N5verb
乗るのる
to ride, to board (a vehicle/boat)
S1E1 — “船ふねに乗のりたいなら”
“If you want to board a ship...”
Thorfinn keeps begging to ride the ship — the verb carries his whole childhood dream.
N4noun
土地とち
land, ground, territory
S1E1 — “もっと豊ゆたかな土地とちがある”
“There is a more bountiful land.”
the search for better land is what pushes every migration in the episode.
N4verb
逃げるにげる
to flee, to escape, to run away
S1E1 — “じゃあ ここからも逃にげたい人ひとは どこに行いくの”
“Then... where do people who want to flee from here go?”
fleeing is the episode's whole theme — the runaway slave, the ancestors who fled Norway, and Thorfinn's closing question.
N3noun
戦士せんし
warrior
S1E1 — “真しんの戦士せんしだけが 身みに着つけられるものなんだ”
“Only a true warrior can wear this.”
"what is a true warrior?" is the central question of Vinland Saga, planted here in episode 1.
